# 🍔 Buddies01 – Buddies Universe

Buddies01 is the official website project of **Buddies Universe**, a food influencer & content creation brand focused on exploring food, culture, and experiences.

This website is designed to represent the **Buddies Universe Instagram food influencer channel**, showcasing the brand identity, journey, and future vision in a clean and modern UI.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

🔗 **Live Link:**  
https://buddiesuniverseofficial.vercel.app

---

## 📸 About Buddies Universe

**Buddies Universe** is a food influence channel active on **Instagram**, created to:
- Explore street food & restaurants  
- Share honest food reviews  
- Build a community around food & experiences  

This website acts as the **official digital presence** of the channel.
